# Payments, plans, subscriptions and retention

Key files: `services/PaymentService.ts`, `services/PaypalService.ts`, `services/PaypalWebhookService.ts`, `services/SubscriptionService.ts`, `services/PlanService.ts`, `cron/paymentReconcile.cron.ts`, `cron/subscriptionStatus.cron.ts`, entities `Payment`, `Plan`, `Subscription`, `PaypalWebhookEvent`.

## Plans

- Admin-managed and **versioned**: a price or duration change creates a new plan row; payments keep pointing at the version they bought. Price must be greater than 0.
- Durations started as 7, 15, 21 and 30 days. The public pricing section and Billing show every active plan in the admin's `sortOrder`, with the admin's `isPopular` flag.
- Plans include **no** lucky-draw spins.

## One-time orders (PayPal Orders API)

1. `PaymentService.initiatePayment(companyId, planId)` creates a `payments` row (`status: pending`, `kind: order`) and a PayPal order.
2. The frontend approves it; `capturePayment(companyId, paypalOrderId)` captures, then inside one transaction marks the payment `captured`, extends `companies.subscription_expires_at` (stacking after the current window), writes the audit row, and queues the receipt email with the invoice PDF.
3. `paymentReconcile` (every 10 min) settles rows stuck in `capturing` for 15+ minutes by asking PayPal, so a timed-out buyer still gets access.
4. Pending rows are abandoned checkouts; they never charged anyone and are left alone.

## Spin add-ons

`initiateSpinPurchase` / `createSpinAddonOrder` with `kind: spin_addon`, `plan_id` null, and `draw_period_key` set to the period the spins belong to. Capture refuses only when the exact period key is no longer open (`LuckyDrawRepository.freeDrawPeriodOpen`). Rules for who may buy are in `lucky-draw.md`.

## Auto-renew (PayPal Subscriptions API)

Fully integrated (`SubscriptionService.subscribe/confirm/applyRemoteState/creditCycle/cancel/changePlan`, webhooks `BILLING.SUBSCRIPTION.*` and `PAYMENT.SALE.COMPLETED`), but **unavailable to users since 2026-09-27** by the owner's decision. Do not run `scripts/sync-paypal-plans.ts` and do not propose re-enabling it. The code stays so it can be switched back on.

- PayPal returns 404 when asked to cancel an `APPROVAL_PENDING` subscription, so duplicates are guarded when the approval is received, not by cancelling.
- `retireAbandonedApprovals` asks PayPal before expiring a pending approval; never expire by age alone.

## Webhooks

`PaymentService.handleWebhook` verifies the signature, inserts into `paypal_webhook_events` (unique on PayPal event id, so retries are no-ops), then `PaypalWebhookService.handle`. Handled: capture completed/denied/refunded/reversed, subscription activated/updated/cancelled/expired/suspended/payment-failed, sale completed. A full refund or reversal removes what was bought (access window or spins) and is audited; a partial refund is flagged for review and changes nothing.

## Invoices and emails

Invoice number `INV-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXXXX`, seller KIMates, no VAT. Generated by `src/pdf/invoice.ts`. Receipt after every payment; renewal-failed and refund-processed emails; plan-ending (24h before) and plan-ended notices with the all-customers and top-10 PDFs attached.

## Mode

`PAYPAL_MODE=sandbox` in production until the owner supplies live credentials. The server logs a warning at boot.

## Retention (`config/retention.ts`)

- `EXPIRY_RETENTION_DAYS = 7`: customer and purchase data is erased 7 days after a plan or trial expires (`ExpiredDataPurgeService`, enabled by `EXPIRY_PURGE_ENABLED=true`).
- `DELETION_GRACE_DAYS = 7`: an account deletion request erases after 7 days (`AccountDeletionService`).
- `lucky_draws` winner rows survive the purge with snapshot columns (migration `1787112000000`).
